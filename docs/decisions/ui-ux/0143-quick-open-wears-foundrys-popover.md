# Quick Open wears Foundry's popover

Decided: 2026-11-15
Status: accepted

Supersedes: ui-ux/0006, only the look of Quick Open's card; its backdrop, keys and focus stand.
Quick Open (⌘K) is a `FoundryPopover` 600 pt wide over the dimmed window: the sheet grey in a 2 pt ink frame casting the hard ink block.
Its search field sits over a 1 pt rule, and each result is a compact Foundry menu row, the bold title with its note beside it; the picked row is the sunk grey.
Why: it is a menu of places and commands, and read as a stock rounded panel next to Foundry's menus.
