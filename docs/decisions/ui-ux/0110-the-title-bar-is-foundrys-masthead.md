# The title bar is Foundry's masthead over a 2 pt ink rule, and its statistics read as a ruled table

Decided: 2026-11-14
Status: accepted

The title bar is 52 pt and ends on a 2 pt ink rule across the window; the panels start 12 pt under it.
Echo's mark is a 22 pt solid ink square casting a 3 pt accent block, beside "ECHO" in the display face (Archivo 800, 75 % wide) at 22 pt.
The search field is up to 460 pt wide and 32 pt tall: the sheet inside a 1 pt rule, with ⌘K in a mono box in the same rule.
The readouts on the right (Workers running, Session, Week, their resets, Worker start) are JetBrains Mono 11 pt bold capitals, muted, with the figures in ink.
The statistics button is three 3 pt squares; its popover "Workers, last 7 days" has a mono label, the title in the display face, the week's facts as rows between 1 pt rules under a 2 pt ink rule, and the profiles as a table with mono, tabular figures between 1 pt rules.
The popover's own frame and arrow are drawn by macOS (ui-ux/0105).
Why: the TitleBar and Main artboards the human approved.
