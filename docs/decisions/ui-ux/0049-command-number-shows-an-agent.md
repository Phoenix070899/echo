# ⌘1 to ⌘9 show the ledger's agents; ⌥⌘1 to ⌥⌘9 show repos

Decided: 2026-10-29
Status: accepted

Supersedes: the ⌘1 to ⌘9 entry in the shortcut lists of ui-ux/0008 and ui-ux/0018.
Each agent header in the ledger carries its number, 1 to 9 from the top, in the quietest ink (the picked one in full ink); an agent past the ninth has no number.
⌘ and that number shows the agent, as clicking its header does; the Session menu lists them by name.
The repo tabs move to ⌥⌘1 to ⌥⌘9.
Shortcuts taken now: ⌘K Quick Open, ⌘N New Orchestrator, ⇧⌘N New Terminal, ⌥⌘N Fan Out a Task, ⌘0 first agent, ⌘1 to ⌘9 agents, ⌥⌘1 to ⌥⌘9 repos, ⌘L ledger, ⇧⌘[ and ⇧⌘] previous and next repo, ⌘D and ⇧⌘D split, ⌘W close pane, ⌥⌘D Changes, ⇧⌘Return send a review, ⇧⌘E Files, ⌘O Open in Editor, ⇧⌘R Reveal in Finder, ⌥⌘C Copy Path.
Why: the human moves between a repo's Orchestrators far more often than between repos.
