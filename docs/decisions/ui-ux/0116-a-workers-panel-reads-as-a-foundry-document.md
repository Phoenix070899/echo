# A Worker's panel reads as a Foundry document: badge, display title, facts, numbered report

Decided: 2026-11-14
Status: accepted

Supersedes: ui-ux/0021's 30 pt title and 19 pt section headings; its full width, 40 pt margins and Markdown body stay.
The header is 24 pt from the top and 40 pt in: "w1 · profile" in mono and the phase as Foundry's badge (ink when done, red when it went wrong, the accent's soft fill when it needs you, dashed when it ended, an ink frame while it works).
The views (Chat, Terminal or Final Report, Changes, Compare) and Stop Worker sit on that row, or under it when the panel is narrow; the title is the display face at 40 pt, up to two lines.
The facts (Model, Worktree or Where, Latest status, Quiet for) are four equal columns under a 1 pt rule, mono labels over 14 pt values, and the header ends on a 2 pt ink rule.
The Final Report starts with a "FINAL REPORT" label; each section is "01 / State" in mono under a 2 pt ink rule; Copy report is an outline at the end.
Changes: "CHANGES" and the mono count on the desk, the diff as a sheet in a 1 pt rule with each file's 36 pt sunk header, a comment as Foundry's callout labelled "Your note · line N", and the review bar under a 2 pt ink rule with 36 pt buttons.
Why: the Worker artboard, kept as tabs as the app has them.
