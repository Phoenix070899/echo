# Outline buttons and the picked segment are framed in 2 pt ink, at two sizes

Decided: 2026-11-14
Status: accepted

Supersedes: ui-ux/0106's 1.5 pt outline and 28 or 32 pt heights, and ui-ux/0107's 1.5 pt frame and single 24 pt height; the rest of both stands.
An outline button is a 2 pt ink frame.
A button is 28 pt tall with 10 pt mono capitals and a 2 pt accent block behind the primary, or taller (34 or 36 pt, in headers and footers) with 11 pt capitals and a 3 pt block.
A segmented choice's picked segment sits in a 2 pt ink frame; compact ones (agent header, Create from) are 24 pt with 10 pt capitals on a 2 pt track, large ones (Worker header, file preview) 28 pt with 11 pt capitals on a 3 pt track.
Why: Foundry's fd-btn-secondary and segmented control, as the artboards draw them.
