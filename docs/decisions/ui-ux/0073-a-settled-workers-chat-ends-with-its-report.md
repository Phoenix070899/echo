# A settled Worker's chat ends with its Final Report card and takes no typing

Decided: 2026-11-12
Status: accepted

Once a Worker settles, its chat's last row is a card with its Final Report, split on State, Changed and Verified as the Final Report view splits it, and its phase in its colour.
Under it, in place of the message box, a line says the Orchestrator gives it its next Task; no Working row shows, whatever its screen still shows.
Why: a settled Worker's terminal was never shown either (its Final Report was); its next work comes through the Orchestrator.
