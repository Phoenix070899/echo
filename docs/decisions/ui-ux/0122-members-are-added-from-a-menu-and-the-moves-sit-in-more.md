# Members are added from an Add Member menu, and the moves and keys sit in a More menu

Decided: 2026-11-14
Status: accepted

Supersedes: ui-ux/0102's palette of chips (dragged or clicked to add a Member), its row of move buttons and its paragraph of keys; the drags of Member rows and every key of ui-ux/0102 stand.
Add Member, in the bar under the tree and as a dashed row after the last Member, opens a menu of the six Actions, the six shipped Profiles (each for the Action it starts) and Custom; the Member is added under the picked one, or at level 1 under the Orchestrator.
The "⋯" More menu holds Duplicate, Remove, Move Up, Move Down, Indent, Outdent and Move To, each with its key beside it, then the keys that only pick or go and the three drags, so the keys are still told and a drag or a key is never the only way (ui-ux/0008).
Why: the always-visible palette and help took a third of the tree's height; the artboard puts both behind two buttons.
Rejected: keeping chips to drag into the tree, which only added what the menu adds.
