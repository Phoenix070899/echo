# The Files column and a previewed file read as Foundry sheets

Decided: 2026-11-14
Status: accepted

Supersedes: ui-ux/0020's 40 pt side margins for rendered Markdown, now 56 pt with 40 pt above; the rest of 0020 stands.
The Files column's head is 40 pt on the desk: the branch in bold mono, then 28 pt icon buttons.
Its search is 30 pt in a 1 pt rule; its tree has no icons, a mono ▸ or ▾ before a folder, 26 pt rows 12 pt in, and fills from edge to edge, the cursor's with a 3 pt accent edge; a folder with changes shows a small square.
The terminal under it starts at a 2 pt ink rule, with a mono ▸, a framed prompt and "TERMINAL" in mono capitals.
A previewed file's header shows the path in bold mono, Rendered | Source | Edit as a large segmented choice, 30 pt icon squares in a 1 pt rule and a 34 pt Insert into terminal; when that does not fit, the path takes a row of its own above them.
Rendered Markdown sets an h1 in the display face at 64 pt over a 2 pt ink rule, an h2 at 34 pt under one, and the body at 15 pt.
Why: the Files and FilePreview artboards.
