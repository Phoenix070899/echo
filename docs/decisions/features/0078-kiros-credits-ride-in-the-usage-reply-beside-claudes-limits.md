# Kiro's credits ride in the usage reply beside Claude's limits, read from a short-lived `kiro-cli acp`

Decided: 2026-11-13
Status: accepted

Supersedes: features/0068's "the usage bar ... stay Claude Code's for now", for Kiro's credits only; Claude's limits, their refresh and their `error` are unchanged, and stats stay as 0068 left them.
The `usage` reply (and the events snapshot's `usage`) keeps Claude's `limits` as they were and appends one entry when Kiro's credits are known: `{"name": "Kiro credits", "percent": 30, "resets": "Dec 1", "detail": "603.62 of 2000"}`. `name`, `percent` and `resets` mean what they mean for Claude's entries (`resets` is null when Kiro gives no date); `detail` is new, optional, and only on this entry, so a reader that ignores it keeps working. `percent` is the used share of the plan's credits, floored as Kiro's own figure is; `detail` is used then plan credits with at most two decimals, as Kiro's TUI prints them.
The source is Kiro's account usage over ACP, found in the bundled server and recorded in `docs/research/kiro-cli-usage-spike.md`: the daemon spawns `kiro-cli acp --agent-engine v3 --auth-method cli` in its own process group, sends `initialize` and then `_kiro/account/getUsage`, skips lines whose id is not the request's, and reads `data.usageBreakdowns` (the `CREDIT` entry: `used`, `limit`) and `data.billingCycleReset` (`YYYY-MM-DD`, shown as "Dec 1"). It never sends `session/new` or a prompt, so no session is made and no credits are spent. The child gets a 60 second limit, its stdin closed, two seconds to exit, then its group is stopped; it never outlives the call.
The child runs with `KIRO_HOME` set to `<state>/kiro-home`, so its log folders do not push the human's own out of `~/.kiro/logs`. The login lives in `~/Library/Application Support/kiro-cli`, not under `KIRO_HOME`. That was verified for IAM Identity Center only: where the login does not work from an empty `KIRO_HOME`, the reading fails and no credits show. It does not fall back to the default home, which would churn the human's logs every five minutes.
The credits are read on a thread of their own, on Claude's cadence (every five minutes while an events stream is open, and on `usage.refresh`) and retry rules (three tries, then the next try a minute sooner than usual), so Claude's refresh is never delayed and Kiro's failures set no `error`. A failed reading, `success: false`, a reply without `data` ("your plan is managed by admin"), no `CREDIT` breakdown, a limit of 0 or `hasLimit: false` keep the last good entry. When `kiro-cli` is not installed or `kiro-cli whoami` exits non-zero, the entry is dropped and nothing is shown, waited on for the whole cadence.
The program is `kiro-cli` on the daemon's PATH, whatever CLI the agents are configured to use. A daemon started with `HUB_TEST_AGENT` reads no Kiro credits unless `HUB_TEST_KIRO` names a stand-in (`crates/hub/tests/fake-kiro-cli.sh`), so `cargo test` never runs a real Kiro.
Why: the human's credits are what runs out; the account figure is what Kiro's own `/usage` shows, and ACP answers it in under two seconds with no side effects.
Rejected: summing the per-turn `usage_summary` credits of Echo's sessions, which misses other machines and other agents and reads 0.04 % of an account that is 30 % spent; a second list or a `kiro` object in the reply, which every consumer would have to learn; an `error` for Kiro in the existing field, which would show a Claude problem on a Kiro failure; falling back to the human's `KIRO_HOME`.
